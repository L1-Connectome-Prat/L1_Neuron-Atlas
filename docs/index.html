L1 Neuron Atlas

An interactive atlas of the first-instar Drosophila larval connectome, in symmetrized PK_L1CNSsym space.

  • Anatomy draws the differentiated and SU neurons of each lineage, their tracts, and the neuropil compartments in 3D.
  • Compartment Connectivity shows the directed connection weights between compartments at Level 1 and Level 2.
  • Lineage Innervation shows the fraction of each lineage's inputs and outputs in every Level 1 and Level 2 compartment, including the SEZ and VNC.

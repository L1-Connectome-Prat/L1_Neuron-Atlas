Connection weight
w(s → t) = Source PSDs × Target T-bars ⁄ Total T-bars
Terms
𝒮
Neurons with at least one postsynaptic density in s and a fragment in t
Source PSDs
Postsynaptic densities those neurons collect in s
Target T-bars
T-bars those neurons contribute in t
Total T-bars
T-bars of every neuron with a fragment in t

Pairs with an empty 𝒮 are omitted. Intrinsic connectivity follows the same rule.

L1 Neuron Atlas

An interactive atlas of the first-instar Drosophila larval connectome, in symmetrized PK_L1CNSsym space.

Views
Anatomy
The differentiated and SU neurons of each lineage, their tracts, and the neuropil compartments in 3D.
Compartment Connectivity
Directed connection weights between compartments at Level 1 and Level 2.
Lineage Innervation
The fraction of each lineage's inputs and outputs in every Level 1 and Level 2 compartment, including the SEZ and VNC.
Use
  • Each view is held in the page address. Copy link on the Anatomy tab records the lineage, colors, and camera; the heatmap tabs record the level and the kept lineage.
  • The color square of a compartment in the tree, or of a group in the legend, sets its color and opacity.
  • On phones and tablets, Controls opens the panel.
